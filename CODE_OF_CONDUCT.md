# Geek Protocol Community Code of Conduct

Geek Protocol exists to make learning more rewarding, competitive, and accessible. Project spaces should reflect that purpose.

## Our standard

Participants are expected to:

- communicate with patience, respect, and intellectual honesty;
- welcome learners and builders of different backgrounds and experience levels;
- critique ideas and code without attacking people;
- credit creative, technical, and community contributions;
- protect private information and responsible vulnerability disclosures; and
- avoid harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized conduct, spam, fraud, or deliberate disruption.

## Scope

This standard applies in repository issues, pull requests, reviews, community spaces, and public interactions where someone represents Geek Protocol.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove content, close contributions, limit participation, or ban accounts when conduct puts people, the project, or its users at risk. Serious threats or illegal activity may be reported to the appropriate service provider or authority.

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner through the contact method listed on the official Geek Protocol website. Security vulnerabilities must follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md), not the conduct process.

Enforcement decisions should be proportionate, documented privately where appropriate, and focused on protecting a constructive learning community.
