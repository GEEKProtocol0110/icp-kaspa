## New tests

`tests/protected_vm.rs` now verifies actual spends of the normal and cold
terminal outputs. Each uses the correct final P2PK key, a different destination,
zero input sequence and transaction locktime, and a fee one sompi above the
previous covenant cap. Wrong-role signatures fail. A third test links actual
transaction IDs, scripts and amounts across initiation, recovery, cold release
and the final P2PK spend. The P2PK transaction has no covenant redeem script.

The fixture keys model possession of the accepted roles; their signatures are
ordinary Schnorr signatures, not generated by a quantum attack. These passing
paths make explicit why the prototype does not establish all-role-compromise
security after terminal exits. They are expected behavior under the documented
narrower model, not a bypass of its pinned payout rules.

Native verification checks scripts and sequence commitments. It does not mine
blocks, establish actual UTXO age, claim network acceptance for new transactions
or evaluate fees/mass under live conditions. There is no new funded run,
induced public-network reorganization, independent-host acceptance or security
audit. The old fee cap is deliberately exceeded only to demonstrate its boundary.

See [SECURITY-TARGET.md](../docs/SECURITY-TARGET.md) for the exact current claim,
paper mapping and proposed design gates. Data tickets, private key delivery,
STARK integration, cold rescue and rotation remain separately specified work.
